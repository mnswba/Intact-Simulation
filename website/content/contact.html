WHO WE ARE

Design and engineering,
from idea to equipment.

URDT — 聯雨設計製造有限公司 — is a design and manufacturing company working across equipment development, semiconductor process technology, robotics and AR/VR applications. Its services connect equipment design, technical verification and integration, with support for bringing systems toward production.

URDT’s published focus includes semiconductor equipment design, integration and maintenance; quadruped robotics, autonomous navigation and mechatronics; and AR smart glasses, computer vision and AI integration.

THE DEVELOPER

Abdul Samad

Intact Simulation was developed by Abdul Samad, a member of URDT and an R&D Engineer at the company. He completed his PhD at National Cheng Kung University (NCKU) in Tainan, Taiwan.

Intact brings conversational AI, CAD construction and measured engineering evidence into one practical design loop: describe an idea, build it, inspect it and refine it with intent.

A USEFUL FIRST MESSAGE

Tell us what matters.

  1. The product or equipment’s purpose and operating context.
  2. Critical dimensions, interfaces and deliverables.
  3. For analysis: the material, loads, supports and desired result.
  4. For an Intact issue: operating system, client and plugin versions, reproduction steps and a redacted error.

Keep confidential CAD, credentials and identity documents out of public messages. Share sensitive project material only through an agreed channel.
