3. Save & review

Exports use the last successful build. STL is a triangle mesh without a unit field; import as millimetres. Curves are approximated.

Scope & privacy

Supports boxes, cylinders/cones, spheres, unions, subtractions and intersections, plus rotation and translation.

No native STEP, FreeCAD features, fillets, simulation, imported meshes or 65-tool desktop parity. Mesh checks do not certify manufacturing or physical performance.

Geometry computation stays in this browser. Models are not uploaded by this app. Hosting receives ordinary page requests; information you paste into ChatGPT is handled by ChatGPT. Save recipes before leaving: browser memory is temporary.

Needs JavaScript, WebAssembly, WebGL and sufficient memory. Older or restricted browsers may not work. Hardware testing on iOS and Android is pending.
