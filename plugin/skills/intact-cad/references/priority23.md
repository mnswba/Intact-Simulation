# Priority 2 and 3 tools in 0.12

Use these tools for their documented domains, not as universal CAD or certified simulation. All distances are CAD XYZ mm; simulation material/load units are explicitly SI. Results carry artifact IDs; a changed design makes older results stale.

- `build_lattice_mm`: Euclidean distance-transform TPMS offset in millimeters. Supply size, cell_size, thickness_mm and samples_per_cell. At least three grid samples are required across the thinnest wall, including gradient. `measure_mesh_quality` measures actual exported volume/bounds and minimum/maximum finite wall chords. Grid uncertainty and sampled wall thickness are not a global thickness proof.
- `refine_lattice`: adapt the global sampling resolution and compare saved volumes/densities. `converged:false` is a real result. This is not spatial octree refinement. Budgets are enforced; never raise them to hide a failure.
- `build_hybrid`: union, difference or intersection between an implicit lattice and a named native part, including imported STEP. Other parts remain. Native CAD is tessellated and the output is mesh geometry. Tiny declared alignment perturbations may be used to avoid export-degenerate coincidences; inspect metadata and quality results. This does not implement a smooth native/implicit blending kernel.
- `convert_mesh_to_cad`: create a separate real native STEP from sewn planar triangular faces, with <=8000 faces and volume rechecking. Faceted STEP is explicitly identified; do not call it fitted smooth analytic TPMS. No silent decimation.
- `set_assembly_joints`: <=11 native parts, acyclic fixed/revolute/slider joints, mm/degrees, explicit axis/origin and min/max values. Joint values become numeric parameters. Updates use the same fixed rest STEP instead of accumulating transforms. Reports test all part pairs for overlap at the posed configuration. No rigid-body dynamics or continuous collision proof.
- `build_freeform`: periodic interpolating spline sections (4–64 points each), native smooth or ruled loft. No general G2 continuity constraints or sculpting.
- `compare_designs`: compare actual volumes, part counts, explicitly assumed mass density and available current reports. No invented performance scores.
- `reconstruct_geometry`: calibrated single-view silhouette extrusion; 2–3 aligned orthographic silhouettes with supplied XYZ dimensions; or closed STL scan import. Always distinguish measured input, supplied depth/scale and inferred silhouettes. No perspective photogrammetry or recovered hidden/internal geometry. Scan meshes remain meshes unless explicitly converted.
- `component_catalog` / `insert_component`: five nominal ISO4762 screw envelopes and SKF608 envelope, with dimension source URLs and declared simplifications. These are not threaded production models or a complete supplier library. Supplier STEP imports use the existing import tool; do not invent manufacturer dimensions.
- `manufacturing_review`: finite draft, CNC visibility and wall samples; screening only. `engineering_drawing`: native-dimensioned orthographic PDF with tessellated edges, no classified hidden lines or full GD&T. `build_sheet_metal`: one real cylindrical bend and a separate neutral-axis blank using user K factor and tangent leg dimensions. No general unfolding or springback.
- `run_3d_simulation`: select one connected native solid and an axis. Minimum/maximum end planes must have planar facets. `thermal`: isotropic steady conduction with prescribed end temperatures and optional volumetric heat source, insulated remainder. `structural`: isotropic small-strain elasticity with clamped minimum plane and a total force uniformly distributed over the maximum plane. `stokes`: the selected CAD solid must represent the **fluid volume**, not a metal wall or hardware. It solves incompressible creeping flow with inlet pressure traction, zero outlet traction and no-slip remaining walls, Taylor-Hood P2/P1 tetrahedra. These are real 3D solvers; no turbulent/inertial CFD, nonlinear/contact mechanics, conjugate heat transfer or multiphysics. Report material assumptions, mesh, BCs, residual, conservation and convergence. Stokes Re estimate must be <=0.1; structural strain must remain <=1% for verification. Stress maxima need their own convergence study. `verified` requires the reported refinement criterion and regime check; never turn an unverified solve into a certification statement.
- `optimize_design`: bounded parameter sweep on separate model copies, preserving the original. Physics objectives use actual converged simulations; infeasible/unverified candidates are excluded. No global optimum or topology optimization. Parameters such as lattice gradient can be swept when they are exposed by the model source.
- `submit_engineering_job`, `get_engineering_job`, `cancel_engineering_job`: detached bounded operations, <=3 active jobs, <=15 minutes overall and separate per-worker limits. Use the corresponding typed tool's settings. Queued edits retain input revision guards. Unchanged feature nodes reuse checksum-verified B-reps, while parameter/dependency changes invalidate downstream nodes. Cancellation preserves prior designs and completed artifacts. Prefer jobs for repeated PDE solves and refinement.

The compact viewer omits the Engineering results & jobs panel and scalar overlay controls. Use engineering_evidence and get_engineering_job to read saved reports, cancel_engineering_job for cancellation, and the returned engineering artifact paths for PDF/VTU results. Report results in the AI conversation and distinguish stale artifacts after CAD changes.
